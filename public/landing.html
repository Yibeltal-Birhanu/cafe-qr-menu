WHY CAFEMENU

Engineered for Cafe Owners, Loved by Guests

Replace dirty, outdated laminated menus with an interactive digital presentation that increases average check size and simplifies daily service.

Zero Reprint Costs

Change prices, adjust seasonal menus, and correct typos on the fly from your admin screen without waiting on expensive print shop turnarounds.

Camera-First QR Access

Guests scan with their native iPhone or Android camera app. Zero app downloads, zero account logins, and zero friction to ordering.

Mouth-Watering Visuals

Diners eat with their eyes first. Showcase appetizing photography, spice tags, and prep times that boost side orders and desserts.

Bilingual Dual-Language

Serve both local patrons and international tourists seamlessly with one-tap switching between English and Amharic (ካንኝና).

Table QR Studio

Generate custom branded table QR stands with table numbers, venue Wi-Fi credentials, and crisp vectors ready for print.

Real-Time Availability

Instantly toggle 86'd items with a single switch. Keep guests informed and stop your waitstaff from taking orders for sold-out dishes.

HOW IT WORKS

From Kitchen to Smartphone in 3 Steps

Getting your cafe live on CafeMenu is simple, fast, and requires zero technical experience.

01

Configure Your Menu

Add your categories, dish descriptions, prices, dietary tags, and photography using our straightforward cafe administration dashboard.

02

Print QR Table Stands

Download high-resolution table QR codes with table numbering and Wi-Fi credentials pre-configured for your dining room.

03

Guests Scan & Enjoy

Customers point their smartphone camera at the table QR code to browse your menu in high resolution and discover your best dishes.
